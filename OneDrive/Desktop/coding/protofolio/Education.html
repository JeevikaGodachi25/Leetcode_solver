Education is a multifaceted process that encompasses formal, non-formal, and informal learning. Formal education occurs in structured institutions such as schools, colleges, and universities, following a systematic curriculum and organized teaching methods. Non-formal education involves planned learning activities outside traditional institutions, such as vocational training, adult literacy programs, and online courses. Informal education is unstructured, arising from daily life experiences, family interactions, social activities, and self-directed learning
